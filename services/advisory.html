Service VI

Advisory

Quarterly conversations about margins, taxes, and what comes next — before you need them.

What's Included

Advisory only works when it's grounded in real numbers. Because we already keep your books, our recommendations come from your actuals, not assumptions.

  • Quarterly strategy calls with your named accountant
  • Budgeting and cash flow forecasting
  • Margin and profitability analysis by service line or product
  • Scenario planning for hiring, pricing, or expansion decisions
  • Entity and tax-structure check-ins as your business grows
  • Exit and succession planning conversations, when the time comes

How We Work

Calls are scheduled quarterly by default, with room for ad hoc conversations whenever a real decision — a hire, a lease, a new line of business — is on the table.

We come to each call with a point of view, not just a recap of the numbers. If something looks off or an opportunity looks worth pursuing, we'll say so.

Our Standard for Handling Your Information

Advisory conversations often cover things you haven't told anyone else yet — an acquisition, a sale, a partner buyout. We treat that accordingly.

  • Strategic discussions held to the same confidentiality as your filings
  • Notes and forecasts stored in your secure portal, never loose email attachments
  • Access limited to your advisory team, never shared without your consent
  • An optional written NDA available for particularly sensitive planning
  • Encrypted storage for all planning documents and forecasts
  • A written confidentiality commitment on every engagement

At a Glance

Quarterly cadence, plus as-needed calls for major decisions. Includes forecasting. Best suited to growth-stage decisions with real financial weight.
