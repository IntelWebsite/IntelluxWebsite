Service III

Tax Preparation

Business and personal returns, prepared by the same team that already keeps your books.

What's Included

Because we already maintain your books, your return is prepared from numbers we've reconciled all year — not reconstructed under deadline pressure in March.

  • Business returns — sole prop, partnership, S-corp, and C-corp
  • Personal returns for owners and their households
  • Proactive tax planning meetings before year-end, not just filing after
  • Prior-year cleanup and catch-up filings
  • Extension management and estimated payment calculations
  • E-filed returns with secure digital signature

How We Work

If we keep your books, filing season starts from clean, closed numbers — no chasing down a year of receipts. If we don't yet, we'll do a focused cleanup of what's needed to file accurately.

We schedule a planning conversation mid-year and again before year-end, so tax moves happen while they can still make a difference — not after the year is already closed.

Our Standard for Handling Your Information

Your tax file contains some of the most personal financial information you have. Here's how we protect it:

  • Tax documents exchanged only through our encrypted secure portal
  • Every return reviewed by a second preparer before it's filed
  • Records retained per current IRS recordkeeping and Circular 230 guidance
  • Secure electronic signature — no printing or emailing sensitive forms
  • Access limited to your preparer and one reviewing accountant
  • A written confidentiality commitment on every engagement
