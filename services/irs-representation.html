Service VII

IRS Representation & Audit Assistance

Notices, audits, and correspondence handled on your behalf, so you never face the IRS alone.

What's Included

A letter from the IRS rarely means what it sounds like it means. Most notices are routine — but they still need a correct, timely response from someone who knows your full financial picture.

  • Direct correspondence with the IRS and state agencies under signed power of attorney
  • Notice review and response — most resolved without you ever calling the IRS
  • Full audit representation from initial notice through resolution
  • Penalty abatement requests where reasonable cause applies
  • Payment plan and offer-in-compromise coordination when needed
  • A single point of contact who already knows your full financial picture

How We Work

Once you sign Form 2848, we become your authorized representative and the agency corresponds directly with us. You send us the notice; we tell you plainly what it means and what, if anything, you need to decide.

We handle the back-and-forth directly and update you at real decision points — not on every piece of routine correspondence.

Our Standard for Handling Your Information

IRS and state matters involve some of your most sensitive financial history. We handle that history with deliberate care.

  • Correspondence handled and stored under strict chain-of-custody practices
  • Power of attorney scoped narrowly to exactly what the matter requires
  • Access restricted to the representing accountant and a reviewing senior partner
  • Documents exchanged through our encrypted secure portal
  • Case notes retained per current IRS recordkeeping guidance
  • A written confidentiality commitment on every engagement

At a Glance

Authorization via IRS Form 2848. Scope runs from simple notices through full audits. One point of contact throughout.
