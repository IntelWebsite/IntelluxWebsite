Payroll

Your people paid precisely and on time, every pay period — filings, deposits, and year-end forms included.

What's Included

Payroll is unforgiving — miss it once and you hear about it immediately. We run it on your schedule, calculate it correctly, and handle every filing that comes with it.

  • Full-service processing on your schedule — weekly, biweekly, or semimonthly
  • Direct deposit, digital pay stubs, and employee self-service access
  • Federal and state tax withholding, deposits, and quarterly filings
  • New-hire reporting and year-end W-2 / 1099 preparation
  • Garnishments, benefits deductions, and multi-state payroll for remote teams
  • A 100% on-time payroll record we stand behind

How We Work

Hours and salary changes come in through a secure portal or your time-tracking system. Your payroll specialist reviews every run before it's funded — not after — so errors get caught before anyone's paid incorrectly.

We track federal and state rate changes and filing deadlines as part of the service, so you're not the one who has to notice when something changes.

Our Standard for Handling Your Information

Payroll touches your team's most personal information — Social Security numbers, bank details, pay history. We treat it accordingly.

  • Employee SSNs, bank details, and pay data encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Payroll data accessible only to your assigned specialist and a reviewing manager
  • A secure employee self-service portal — no payroll data sent by email or text
  • Deposit and filing confirmations retained and available to you at any time
  • An immediate correction process if a discrepancy is ever flagged
  • A written confidentiality commitment on every engagement

At a Glance

On-time record: 100%. Federal and state filings handled every quarter. Multi-state payroll supported for distributed teams.
