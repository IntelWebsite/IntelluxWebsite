Service IV

Financial Reporting

Elegant monthly statements with commentary you'll actually read — and numbers you can actually act on.

What's Included

Numbers without context don't help anyone make a decision. Every report we send comes with plain-English commentary on what changed and why it matters.

  • Monthly profit & loss, balance sheet, and cash flow statements
  • Plain-English commentary highlighting what changed and why
  • Custom KPI dashboards built around what matters in your industry
  • Board- and lender-ready report packages
  • Year-over-year and budget-to-actual comparisons
  • Quarterly benchmarking against your own prior performance

How We Work

Your reports are generated directly from the reconciled books at monthly close, so the numbers in your dashboard always match the numbers in your filings — no separate spreadsheet, no version drift.

Each month, your accountant walks you through what moved and what it means in a short review — five minutes if that's all you need, longer if something's worth discussing.

Our Standard for Handling Your Information

Your reports often reach a wider circle than your day-to-day bookkeeping — partners, lenders, board members. We keep that circle deliberate.

  • Reports delivered through your secure client portal, not open email threads
  • Dashboard access limited to the people you specifically designate
  • A standardized, versioned chart of accounts so historical comparisons stay accurate
  • Source data encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Reports retained and available to you at any time, not just at delivery
  • A written confidentiality commitment on every engagement

At a Glance

Delivered monthly, by the 10th. PDF package plus a live dashboard. Commentary included on every report, not just numbers.
